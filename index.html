Two families. Different configurations.

Now, meet the candidates.

These processor families overlap in what they can do. The useful question is which specific laptop configuration matches your needs and budget.

Explore for everyday needs

Intel® Core™ Series 3

Intel positions Core Series 3 for value-focused computing, including everyday use, schools and small businesses.

  • Worth exploring for study, browsing, documents, video calls and common productivity.
  • Specific models differ in frequency, graphics, connectivity and other capabilities.
  • Check the whole laptop's price, memory, battery reviews and display—not just the processor.

Explore for demanding workflows

Intel® Core™ Ultra Series 3

Core Ultra Series 3 includes a range of configurations, including higher-end options aimed at demanding graphics, gaming, creation and AI-enabled workloads.

  • Worth comparing for graphics-heavy projects, demanding multitasking, gaming or AI-related work.
  • Capabilities vary considerably by exact model and laptop design.
  • Check software compatibility, memory, cooling, battery results and price for the system you're considering.
